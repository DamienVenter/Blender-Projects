## Verification against his photos (reference/damien-bike-2026-11-12/08 and 09, his chest)
Crops turned upright (body vertical) and enlarged; the chest LEATT of Leatt's print used as the ruler.
- **BikeWise**: the box above the LE of the chest LEATT shows a big black BIKE with a small vertical WISE to its
  right and a dark band along the bottom, on white with a white margin: the official logo (black on white), as is.
- **Pirelli** (not on Damien's list, but printed on the jersey): the yellow box above the brain icon reads PIRELLI in
  red with the long P in photo 08 (09 too blurred to read): Pirelli's own yellow box logo. Decided on the lettering
  (08) and the colours (yellow box, red letters); it cannot be the Cycleworx logo (that one is red, blue and black).
- **USN**: under the pink yoke, his right of the centre chevron: the atom mark and USN in navy, with a white contour
  round the letters on the black jersey (08, clear), as the official logo plus a white outline.
- **Motul**: under USN: a red box with white letters ending ...TUL (09): the official white-on-red Motul.
- **Cycleworx Suspension**: under the yoke, his left of the centre chevron: a red C, then blue and dark letters on a
  white contour (09, 08): the official logo (its CWORX mark; the logo's thin SUSPENSION line, 9 % of the mark's
  height, sits under it). Under that there is one more light band with dark lettering, about as wide as CWORX and
  0.8 of its height (08; smaller in 09), not legible. It is too tall to be the logo's SUSPENSION line, so it is not
  printed: question for Damien.
