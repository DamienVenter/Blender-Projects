# Photo checklist for Damien

How the photos are taken matters as much as what they show. Good photos are what let the model be exact.

## How to send
- Send **original files**. WhatsApp compresses photos and video heavily. Best: put them in a Google Drive folder and tell Claude the folder name (Claude can pull them from Drive). Next best: upload them straight into the chat.
- Name or group them by part if you can (e.g. `bike_left_profile.jpg`). Not required.

## General technique
- **Light**: overcast day or open shade. Harsh sun hides detail in black shadows. One extra set in direct sun is useful for how paint and plastics reflect.
- **Lens**: stand back and zoom in (2x or 3x on a phone) instead of standing close. This removes the wide-angle distortion that makes measurements wrong.
- **Scale**: put a tape measure or a 1 m ruler on the ground next to the bike, in line with it, in the profile shots.
- **Level**: phone held level, not tilted. For profile shots, the phone at axle height (about the middle of the wheel hub).
- **Sharp and complete**: whole subject in frame, in focus. Several slightly different shots are better than one.

## 1. Bike (your 2026 CRF250R, exactly as it is now)
Bike upright on its wheels on flat level ground, no rider (someone holding it upright or a stand that keeps the wheels on the ground). Front wheel pointing straight.

**Orthographic set (most important)**
- [ ] Left side profile: 8 to 10 m away, zoomed, camera at axle height, square to the bike
- [ ] Right side profile: same
- [ ] Front: straight on, at axle height, then one from front number plate height
- [ ] Rear: straight on, same heights
- [ ] Top: from a ladder or balcony, straight down if possible
- [ ] Four 3/4 views (front-left, front-right, rear-left, rear-right)

**Close-ups (fill the frame with each part)**
- [ ] Cockpit from the rider's seat: bars, bar pad, clamps, grips, levers, perches, kill switch, map switch, master cylinder, cables and hoses
- [ ] Number plate front and back of plate
- [ ] Triple clamps top and bottom, steering stem nut
- [ ] Forks: tubes, lower legs, fork guards, axle and pinch bolts, air/adjuster caps
- [ ] Front brake: caliper both sides, disc (rotor) pattern, hose routing
- [ ] Front wheel: hub both sides, spokes and nipples, rim (brand stickers), tyre sidewall text, tread pattern (straight on to the tread)
- [ ] Radiator shrouds both sides, radiator louvres, radiator guards
- [ ] Fuel tank cap and breather hose
- [ ] Seat: top, sides, cover texture and seams, close-up of the grip pattern
- [ ] Side panels both sides, airbox area, rear fender top and underside, rear number plates
- [ ] Engine left side: every cover, shift lever, front sprocket area, case guards
- [ ] Engine right side: every cover, water pump, brake pedal, case guards
- [ ] Cylinder head, top of engine, wiring and hoses visible between frame rails
- [ ] Frame: welds, frame guards, footpegs (both, including teeth), peg mounts
- [ ] Exhaust: header pipe (colour/heat marks), mid-pipe, muffler, end cap, mounting brackets
- [ ] Chain, both sprockets (count the teeth if easy), chain guide, chain slider, chain adjusters
- [ ] Swingarm both sides, linkage underneath, shock (spring colour, reservoir, adjusters)
- [ ] Rear brake: caliper, disc, disc guard, master cylinder and reservoir
- [ ] Rear wheel: hub both sides, sprocket bolts, tyre sidewall and tread
- [ ] Skid plate / engine guard underneath
- [ ] Every sticker and decal as it is now, straight on
- [ ] Wear: scratches, scuffs, faded plastic, dirt in corners (this is the detail that sells realism)

**Information**
- [ ] List of every non-stock part (bars, grips, pipe, wheels, tyres, brakes, guards, seat cover, graphics, plates, pegs, sprockets)
- [ ] Tyre brand, model and sizes
- [ ] Bike colour and graphics as it should look in the animation (the new sticker kit references, when ready)
