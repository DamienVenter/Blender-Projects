# Leatt gear: official reference images

Damien's riding gear, identified in `motocross-whip/research/rider-gear-leatt.md` (read that first: it has the
identification, how each item was checked against his photos, colours, logo placement, construction and sizes).

- Downloaded 2026-11-12. Every file is the original as published (exact bytes, no re-encoding), at the largest
  size offered: 1920 px from Leatt US (Shopify CDN), 1600 px from the NZ distributor.
- Leatt US (us.leatt.com) is the only Leatt site that answers from the build server; leatt.com and the `za`
  site redirect to it, `eu`, `uk` and `au` are unreachable. On 2026-11-12 the 2026 items are listed as clearance
  (`sale-my26`, tagged "MY26 Obsolete"), so these pages may disappear: the images are kept here.
- File names: `<item>_<colourway>_<view>_<source>_<Leatt id>_<px>.<ext>`.
- 57 files, 34.3 MB, plus the comparison sheets below.

The Velocity 5.5 frame is the same in every colourway (Leatt US handles goggle-velocity-5-5-*: 70 images, all the
same five angles: ISO, Front (the ISO angle), Back, Exploded, lens). Leatt publishes no front, top or side view of
the goggle alone, and no dimensions but the 45 mm strap and the 48 mm Roll-Off film; the MY27 Moto 8.5 kit
(product moto-8-5-helmet-kit, "Velocity 5.5 goggles are included") shows the goggle on the helmet from four sides:
those are the measured views (cameras fitted to the helmet shell, `scripts/rider/goggle.py`). Round 3 also fitted
a camera to the ISO itself (the goggle's own silhouette): the frame's cross-section, fins, beak and posts come from
it (research/rider-ledger/goggle.md).
