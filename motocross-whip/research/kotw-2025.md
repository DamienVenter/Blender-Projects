# KOTW 2025: physical setting research

Researched 2026-11-06 for the motocross whip animation (brief: whip over a 75 ft gap, steel FMX ramp to dirt landing).
Reference photos saved in `reference/kotw2025/` (list in section 8).

Confidence tags used on every fact:
- **[C]** confirmed by two or more independent sources
- **[S]** single source
- **[I]** inferred (from photos, EXIF data, satellite imagery or calculation; the reasoning is given)

Copyright note: the saved photos belong to their photographers (Eric Palmer, Michel Bega / The Citizen, Darryl Hammond / Sowetan) and to the YouTube channels. They are for private modelling reference only. Do not publish them in a shared artifact.

---

## 1. Summary

"King of the Whip 2025" is almost certainly the **2025 Volkswagen Amarok King of the Whip**, held on **Saturday 29 November 2025** at **Montecasino, Fourways, Johannesburg, South Africa**. It is South Africa's biggest FMX event, organised by Ryan van der Spuy of LW Mag. [C]

The jump matches the brief closely:
- **One steel FMX kicker ramp** (sides wrapped in black Monster Energy branding) to **one large sculpted dirt landing** (tan / ochre sand). [C, photos from three photographers]
- The competition jump is described as **23 metres** ("the full-size, twenty-three-metre competition ramp"). 23 m = **75.5 ft**, so the 75 ft brief fits. [S for 2025; the same 23 m figure appears for 2018, 2019 and 2022, see section 4]

The setting is **urban, not rural**: a paved open lot (interlocking concrete pavers) at Montecasino, a huge temporary black-scaffold grandstand on one side, the Tuscan-village facades and towers of Montecasino on the other, sponsor flags, floodlight poles, two VW Amarok bakkies and a Bobcat loader parked under the gap. The contest ran from late afternoon (about 16:00) into the night; the Best Whip final was at night under floodlights with pyrotechnics and a Monster Energy laser show. [C]

### Schedule (as published before the event)
From [Briefly][bri] [S], partly confirmed by [biz] and [zab1] (gates 14:30, action 16:00, finals at night with pyrotechnics) [C]:
- 13:00 VIP / VVIP early access, rider meet-and-greet
- 14:30 gates open, Pit Walk for all ticket holders
- 16:00 official rider introductions, then qualifying through the afternoon into early evening
- 18:00 freestyle motocross show, then semi-finals
- Best Trick contests, then Monster Energy "Rig Riot" laser, light and pyro show on the Montecasino facade
- 20:30 Best Whip finals
- 21:00 crowning of the King
- 22:00 doors close

### Format and judging [C]
- Best Whip: four qualifying rounds, 16-rider semi-final, top 8 final. Judged on **amplitude, style and variation** (Fourways Review says "style, amplitude and precision"). [SA Music News][samn], [Kempton Express][kemp], [zab3]
- Best Trick: Right-Side-Up and Upside-Down categories, three attempts each, run before the Best Whip final. [samn], [kemp]
- Prize purse R250,000. [C]

### Field and results [C]
- 30 riders: 21 South African plus 9 international (Julien Vanstippen BEL, Wyatt Fontenot USA, Pat Bowden AUS, Genki "Wanky" Watanabe JPN, Vicki Golden USA, Ry Hazell AUS, Marc Pinyol ESP, Julien Mannon FRA, Stav Orland UGA). [ewn], [samn]
- **Best Whip (King of the Whip):** 1 Wyatt Fontenot (USA, R60,000), 2 Julien Vanstippen (BEL, R40,000, champion 2022 to 2024), 3 Ry Hazell (AUS, R20,000), 4 Stav Orland (UGA), 5 Vicki Golden (USA, first woman to compete at KOTW). Other finalists: Genki Watanabe, Ryan Adler, Trent Valsecchi. [kemp], [Fourways Review][fr25], [Dirt and Trail][dt]
- **Best Trick Right-Side-Up:** 1 Pat Bowden (Christ air stage dive, no-hander lander), 2 Genki Watanabe ("Octopus"), 3 Julien Vanstippen. [samn], [kemp]
- **Best Trick Upside-Down:** 1 Pat Bowden (Rowedova backflip to one-hander lander), 2 Marc Pinyol (California roll body varial), 3 Julien Vanstippen (backflip tsunami). [samn], [kemp]
- Inaugural **Ricky Raaff Award**: Ryan Adler (crashed heavily, repaired his bike and rode again). [yam], [kemp]
- Notable: 12-year-old Ryan Boggett jumped the full-size competition ramp at least five times. [dt], [cit]
- Kempton Express says Fontenot and Hazell ride for Stark Varg (electric). [S] [kemp]

### What the ramp looks like (from photos 01, 04, 05, 07, 12) [I]
- Classic steep FMX kicker: straight run-up section, curved transition, kicked lip at the top.
- Both **side skirts are covered in black wrap** (banner fabric or vinyl, draped to the ground) with a large **Monster Energy** claw logo and "MONSTER ENERGY" text, and a **white outline stripe** following the deck edge.
- **Deck**: dark grey / black with a grid-like grip texture (looks like expanded metal or a grip mat), with **white bands along both deck edges**.
- No visible dirt pad or wooden approach boards: the run-in is on the paving.
- Height: no figure published. In photo 01 the lip is higher than the Bobcat loader parked beside it, but perspective makes this unreliable. Needs measurement.

### What the landing looks like (photos 01, 03, 04, 10, 11, 16, 19) [I]
- A big **truncated, sculpted mound** with **flat, trimmed faces** (machine-cut planes, not a natural hill). No vegetation.
- Colour: **tan / ochre / yellow-brown sandy soil**, slightly lighter on dry crests, with visible tool and bucket marks on the faces.
- The **face toward the ramp** carries a white banner: "AMAROK" (black on white) above a large "KING OF THE WHIP" wordmark (white letters, red and black outline) with a white mascot graphic to the left, two black round "VW Commercial Vehicles" logos at the upper corners, and a green Monster Energy claw below.
- The **south side face** carries white boards: GOSCOR, azagneiss Sand & Aggregates, Cloete's Sand & Stone. The north side face has a white board too (content not legible).
- The landing downslope (far side, away from the ramp) is **not visible in any photo found**. Its angle and length are unknown.
- Scale: in photo 19 (night podium in front of the ramp-facing face) the mound is several times the height of the people standing in front of it. A rough guess is at least 4 to 5 m high, but this is low confidence.

## 5. Site layout and surroundings

### Orientation (inferred, but consistent across all evidence) [I]
Reasoning: (a) photos taken from the side opposite the grandstand show Tuscan buildings behind the camera's position, and photos taken from the grandstand side show the Tuscan buildings behind the jump; (b) photo 13 looks past the end of the grandstand over a tree line to distant Fourways buildings; (c) in afternoon photos, shadows fall away from the ramp side and the ramp-facing landing face is sunlit; (d) the only open space at Montecasino big enough, next to the Tuscan facades, is the paved north lot (section 7).
- **Jump axis about WNW to ESE** (bearing roughly 105°): ramp at the west end, landing at the east end.
- **Grandstand along the north side**, backing onto the tree line beside Witkoppen Road.
- **Tuscan facades, clock tower, Rig Riot stage and general standing area along the south side.**
- **Start area and pits at the west end**; modern buildings and trees behind the landing at the east end.

### Ground [C, photos 08, 13, 20]
- Interlocking **concrete paving blocks** (zigzag interlocking pavers), buff / tan / light terracotta colour, laid over the whole arena and pits. Loose dirt patches near the ramp and landing.

### Grandstand [C, photos 02, 08, 11, 22]
- One very large **temporary grandstand** running the length of the course: **black scaffold structure**, black seating tiers (about 20 rows or more), **three stair aisles**, **black shade-cloth screening** along the top rail and down the sides.
- Along the top edge: tall poles with **LED floodlights** (about 10 to 14 visible) and a row of **feather / teardrop flags**: navy "VW Commercial Vehicles", maroon "Amarok", black "Garmin", red "Motul", black "Fox", navy "playTSOGO", a green **Monster Energy claw** on a pole at the centre, and blue **Yamaha** flags at the west end.
- Ticket tiers mention a "VIP Grandstand: prime position centre of grandstand". [S] [Howler 2026 page][howler26]

### Barriers and course floor [C, photos 01, 02, 08, 09, 13]
- Base of the grandstand: **orange barrier jackets** printed with the VW logo, "Commercial Vehicles" and "Amarok"; black **FOX** banners; navy **playTSOGO** banners.
- Spectator side: **blue Yamaha / Fox barriers**; start area: blue **Yamaha Racing** barriers.
- Parked under the gap, between ramp and landing: **two VW Amarok double-cab bakkies** (one **dark grey**, one **blue**, both with "King of the Whip" graphics on the doors) and a **white Bobcat skid-steer loader with orange wheel rims** (Goscor / Bobcat branding). Small black ground spotlights in front of the vehicles, black PA speakers on stands, a black Monster Energy flag backdrop near the ramp, black Monster Energy umbrellas and gazebos.

### South side [C, photos 01, 03, 04, 05, 16 to 18, 21]
- Montecasino's **Tuscan-village facades**: sandstone block and ochre / cream plaster walls, crenellated walls, arches, small windows, **terracotta tile roofs**.
- Three distinct towers visible from the arena:
  1. a **cream stucco clock tower** with a clock face and open belfry (photo 01, 03),
  2. a **stone tower with crenellations and a pointed spire** (photos 04, 05, behind the landing),
  3. a **stone bell tower with arched openings and a terracotta dome with a spire** (photo 21).
- **Monster Energy "Rig Riot" stage**: a multi-level black scaffold structure (VIP deck with a "Sanctuary" sign) lined with green LED bars and Monster banners, a large **LED video screen** on a black truss roof, a black Monster Energy **inflatable dome / arch** with graffiti print, CO2 jets, lasers and pyrotechnics. [C: photos 16 to 18, Citizen, ZA Bikers, Dirt and Trail]
- General standing crowd behind orange VW and blue playTSOGO barriers.

### East end, behind the landing [I, photos 03 to 07, 12]
- Modern 3 to 4 storey beige / grey and red-brick buildings (hotel / offices), medium-size broadleaf trees (dark green) to the north-east, white and blue gazebo tents.

### West end, start area and pits [I, photos 13, 20]
- Monster Energy truss arch (rider entry), flame cannons (pyro), Yamaha and Fox barriers, Garmin branding, a white pickup, Monster Energy inflatable domes and team gazebos (Yamaha, Bridgestone) in the pits.
- Behind: tree line and a distant view of Fourways buildings and low hills.

## 7. Satellite view

Imagery: Esri World Imagery, Vantor WorldView-3, captured **7 February 2026** (about 10 weeks after the event, so no event structures are visible). Saved as `reference/kotw2025/24_...`, `25_...` and the annotated `26_...`.

- North of Montecasino's main building lies a **large paved open lot**, roughly **195 m (WNW to ESE) by 105 m**, surfaced in light beige pavers. Its long axis runs about 105° / 285°, parallel to Witkoppen Road. [I, measured on the imagery]
- **North edge**: a continuous belt of trees along Witkoppen Road (R564), with a footpath. **South edge**: the casino's Tuscan buildings with terracotta roofs, and a large white-roofed hall at the south-west corner. **East edge**: a long grey-roofed modern building and a landscaped courtyard. **West edge**: lawns and woodland of the Montecasino Bird Gardens. [I]
- OpenStreetMap marks this lot as parking. Event listings refer to a "Montecasino Outdoor Event Area". [S]
- This lot is the only open area at Montecasino big enough for a 23 m FMX jump with a grandstand, and its surroundings match every 2025 photo (trees to the north and north-east, Tuscan buildings to the south, modern buildings east, Fourways skyline seen to the north-west). It is very probably the KOTW arena, but no source names it explicitly. [I]

## 9. Conflicts with the brief

1. **Gap: no conflict.** 23 m = 75.5 ft. But it is not stated how the 23 m is measured (lip to knuckle, or lip to touchdown). [S]
2. **Steel ramp: matches in type, differs in look** from Damien's practice-site ramps (rust-brown expanded-metal decks, open black frames, several ramps side by side). The KOTW ramp is a **single ramp** with **black branded side wraps** and a dark deck with white edge bands. [C photos]
3. **Dirt landing: matches in type, differs in look.** KOTW's landing is a **machine-sculpted mound of tan sand on a paved car park**, with flat trimmed faces and a sponsor banner. It is not a natural vegetated hill like the practice site. [C photos]
4. **Setting: urban casino arena,** not grassland: pavers, a large grandstand, Tuscan buildings, vehicles, flags and floodlights. [C]
5. **Time of day must be chosen.** Qualifying and early rounds were in late-afternoon sun (16:00 to 18:46), but the **Best Whip final was at night** (about 20:30) under floodlights with pyro and green Monster lighting. [C]
6. **Branding.** Recreating it "exactly" means reproducing real trademarks (VW, Amarok, Monster Energy, Yamaha, Fox, Garmin, Motul, playTSOGO, KOTW wordmark). The KOTW mascot to the left of the wordmark is a white crowned figure that is not legible in the available photos; check it against the repo rule "no skull, demon or occult imagery" before modelling it.
7. **Bike.** The brief's Honda 250 is fine (riders used Yamaha, KTM, Ducati, Husqvarna, Honda and Stark Varg bikes). No conflict, just noting the winner rode a Stark Varg according to one source. [S]
