# 2026 Honda CRF250R: specifications and reference sources

Research date: 2026-11-06. Scope: stock 2026 CRF250R (four-stroke MX, not the RX, not the Works Edition).
Every value below carries a source. Where sources disagree, see "Conflicts". Where nothing reliable exists, see "Gaps".

## 0. Key findings in one place

- **The 2026 CRF250R is mechanically and visually identical to the 2025 model.** Honda's 2026 US release lists no changes ("NEW FOR" section absent), Dirt Rider: "it's the exact same", Dirt Bike magazine: Honda "left almost everything exactly the same" (only the price rose by USD 100). The big redesign (70% new frame, new bodywork, new suspension internals, new exhaust and airbox) happened for **2025**.
- **Honda's 2026 US studio photos are the same photographs as the European 25YM press photos.** I compared all seven matching views pixel by pixel (mean absolute difference 0.5 to 1.7 grey levels out of 255, no decal differences), so the public 4000x2250 European files stand in for the US 2026 files, which need a media login.
- **Rake is market-dependent.** The 2025-generation owner's manual lists caster 28°24' for ED (Europe) and U (Australia) types and 27°33' for GS, LA and MX types; the US sheets say 27.5° (2025) and 27.6° (2026). 27°33' = 27.55°, so the US and GS/LA/MX figures agree. **We need Damien's market (VIN) to pick the rake.** Trail is 117 mm everywhere.
- **Do not use 2027 imagery.** The 2027 model has a new red/white/blue "tricolor" HRC livery, a slightly longer muffler and a smaller airbox-cover opening (US 2027 release).
- Model codes: US 2026 = **CRF250RT** (Honda NZ parts catalogue title "2026 CRF250RT in EXTREME RED"), parts prefix **K95**, colour **Extreme Red R292R**. Example 2026 VIN from a dealer listing: JH2ME1231TK801570 (10th character T = 2026) ([auction123 listing](https://showroom.auction123.com/vern_eide_powerhouse/inventory/21013/JH2ME1231TK801570.html)).
- Europe skipped a "26YM": Honda Europe sold the 25YM through 2026 and launched the 27YM (new graphics only) on 28 May 2026 ([Honda UK spec page](https://www.honda.co.uk/motorcycles/range/off-road/crf250r/specifications.html) lists only "CRF250R 2025" and "CRF250R 2027").

## 3. What changed in 2025 and 2026, and regional differences

**2025 (the redesign), from US25F and EU25:**
- Main frame 70% new parts: +8% torsional rigidity, +5% torsional/lateral ratio, +4% vertical torsional rigidity; new front down tube, stiffener spars, side gusset, pivot plates, upper shock mount, tension bracket.
- Subframe mounting moved from the spars to the pivot plates. Identification cue: two-piece subframe mounting bracket (DR).
- New steering stem, top and bottom clamps, outer fork tubes, front axle and axle clamp.
- Fork: new springs, kit-style Bending Control Valves, seals, oil. Shock: new spring, reservoir, shaft, seal. One-piece linkage, new ratio. Shock removal no longer needs the subframe off.
- Front caliper: new pistons and seal grooves, new machined accents.
- Straighter airbox path, triangle-shaped air filter (DR), more rigid crank, straighter header and muffler, new ECU maps, new handlebar map/HSTC switch.
- New bodywork: one-piece radiator shrouds, flat-surfaced shrouds and side plates, wider side-plate profile, new "CRF family" graphics, Honda wing on the front fender (EU25).
- Swingarm unchanged (EU25).

**2026:** no changes (US26F has no "new for" section; DR, DBM). US price up USD 100 (DR).

**2027 (for contrast only, do not model):** new red, white and blue "tricolor" livery; smaller airbox-cover opening; new, slightly longer muffler; new ECU settings; new fork fluid (US27F). Honda Europe 27YM: new graphics, "CRF250R HRC-WE" replaces the RWE (EU27).

## 4. Exact colour scheme and graphics (2025 and 2026, all markets)

From the studio photos (section 6) and the 2026 Dirt Rider photos:
- **Plastics:** Extreme Red (Honda code R292R, from NZ part numbers such as 83510-K95-AG0ZA "COVER A,R *R292R*"). Front fender, front number plate, shrouds, upper side plates, rear fender and fork guards are red.
- **Radiator shrouds:** decal with large white stacked letters "C", "R", "F" over a diagonal split panel (red into navy blue), "250R" in white below, and the HRC "Honda Racing" logo (red, white, blue). Black radiator louvres show through the shroud vents.
- **Side plates:** red upper part with white "HONDA" lettering just below the seat; lower front part is black textured plastic (fine raised grip pattern) with a recessed air vent.
- **Front fender:** white Honda wing with "HONDA" on the top, near the front (see `studio_front` and `studio_top`).
- **Rear fender:** white "HONDA" on the top near the tail; a white warning label on the fender top (see `studio_top`).
- **Seat:** red cover with a fine texture, no logo.
- **Bar pad:** red Renthal pad with "RENTHAL" in white, repeated. Handlebar black. Grips black.
- **Metal finishes:** frame, subframe, swingarm and triple clamps bare silver aluminium (visible welds on frame); fork outer tubes gold; engine cases silver-grey with "HONDA" cast on the clutch cover; cylinder head cover black; header pipe with bronze/gold heat colour; muffler silver aluminium; rims black with white "D.I.D DirtStar" logo; spokes and nipples silver; front disc guard black with a hexagon mesh window; skid plate black plastic.
- The Dirt Rider test bike carries non-stock black "21" number backgrounds (press bike), so do not copy its plates.

## 10. Conflicts between sources and which to trust

1. **Rake:** US26 27.6°, US25 27.5°, OM 27°33' (GS/LA/MX) and 28°24' (ED/U), EU25/UK/ZA26 28°24'. All official. They are market variants, not errors. Trust OM for the variant; for a US-market bike use 27.6° (27°33' = 27.55°). **Action: get Damien's VIN or market.**
2. **Length:** 2179 mm (all metric sources, OM) vs 85.9 in = 2182 mm (US25, US26) vs 85.8 in (US27). Trust 2179 mm.
3. **Width:** 827 mm vs 32.6 in (828 mm). Trust 827 mm.
4. **Weight:** US curb 234 lb (106.1 kg) with fuel; EU "kerb 102 kg (107 kg wet)", EU25 text calls 102 kg dry; AU kerb 107 kg; magazines 223 lb dry and 232 lb wet. For physics use about 106 to 107 kg ready to ride.
5. **Fuel:** 6.3 L (metric sources) vs 1.7 gal (6.44 L). Trust 6.3 L.
6. **Displacement/stroke:** 249.3 / 249.4 / 249.43 cc; 50.9 vs 50.887 mm. Trust OM (249.43 cc, 50.887 mm).
7. **Handlebar positions:** US "four positions with a range of 26 mm"; EU "26 mm between holder locations plus 10 mm when the holder is turned". Measure on the bike.
8. **Seat height:** 955 mm claimed vs 952.5 mm measured by DR (sag and tyre pressure dependent). Model to 955 mm unloaded.
9. **Triple clamp offset:** 22 mm from aftermarket listings only; other listings mention 20 mm (48 mm forks) and 23 mm (older models). Low confidence until measured.

## 11. Gaps that need measurement on Damien's bike

Priority order for modelling:
1. **Market / VIN** (decides rake 27.6° vs 28°24'), and every non-stock part.
2. Real tyre outside diameters (front and rear), axle heights from the ground, static sag.
3. Fork: outer tube diameter, tube height above the top clamp, axle-to-top-clamp length; triple clamp offset; stem and clamp shapes.
4. Shock eye-to-eye length, spring colour, reservoir size; linkage lengths.
5. Swingarm pivot position (height and distance to the front axle), and what the 585.2 mm swingarm figure measures.
6. Footpeg fore-aft position and peg size (height 422 mm is known).
7. Handlebar bend code, width (cut or not), rise, sweep, clamp position used.
8. Wheels: rear spoke count (front 36 is low confidence), hub diameters and flange spacing, rim profile width, axle diameters.
9. Brake discs: bolt count, petal count, thickness; rear disc guard shape.
10. Chain link count; sprocket teeth confirmation (13/50 stock).
11. Front number plate and side panel outlines; airbox and vent sizes.
12. Muffler length and diameter, header routing points, end cap.
13. Exact decals on Damien's bike (decision 6 says a custom kit will replace stock graphics).
